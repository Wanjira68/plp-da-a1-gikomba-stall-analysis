## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing. She needs to identify sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, determine which products need restocking, and understand the gross profit margins of her products. This analysis uses two weeks of daily sales data and current stock information to help her make better business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales growth:** Total sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing 10.66% growth.
2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, which was 82.50% higher than average weekday sales of KES 2,685.
3. **Stock and margins:** Tomatoes and sukuma need restocking. Avocado recorded the highest gross profit margin at 42.86%.
